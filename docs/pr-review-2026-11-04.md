## Dependencies and validation

Removed forced zod major overrides attached to PRs. Upgraded official x402
packages to 2.29.0 and Hono to 4.13.16 with a regenerated lockfile.

Regression tests cover independently reproduced HMAC, a former hash collision,
malformed cookies, spoofed scheme headers, insufficient verified payment,
cache isolation/bounds, receipt expiry/overwrite, duplicate settlement accounting,
all-open RPC circuits, malformed backend counts and concurrent readiness probes.

All stores remain process-local and reset on restart. No real-chain payment,
production webhook delivery or actual distributed backend was exercised.
